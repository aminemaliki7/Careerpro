## The Precision Targeting Strategy

Smart job seekers don't guess. They **aim with laser precision.**

### Step 1: Pick Your Target Jobs
Instead of applying to 50 random positions, identify 10-15 roles that truly fit your career goals.

**Focus on:**
- Companies you actually want to work for
- Roles that match 70%+ of your existing skills
- Positions with clear growth potential

### Step 2: Analyze What They Really Want
Every job posting has a hidden code. The keywords, requirements, and preferred qualifications tell you exactly how to position yourself.

**Look for:**
- Skills mentioned multiple times
- Specific software or tools they use
- Years of experience they emphasize
- Industry certifications they value

### Step 3: Optimize Your Resume Like a Sniper
This is where most people fail. They think changing a few words is enough.

**Real optimization means:**
- Using their exact terminology
- Quantifying achievements that match their needs
- Restructuring sections to highlight relevant experience
- Ensuring ATS compatibility
