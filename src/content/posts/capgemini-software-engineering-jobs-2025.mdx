## Why Capgemini is Hiring Software Engineers Right Now

Capgemini is one of the world's leading technology transformation partners, and they're **actively mass-hiring for their IT divisions in 2025**. For software engineering graduates, this means access to Fortune 500 clients, cutting-edge projects, and global career opportunities.

The timing couldn't be better - their graduate programs are launching in early 2025 with a focus on fresh talent.

## What They're Looking For

### Key Roles Available
- **Frontend Developers** - Heavy focus on React and JavaScript
- **Full-Stack Engineers** - End-to-end application development  
- **Systems Analysts** - Bridge between technical and business needs
- **Quality Engineers** - Automated testing and DevOps

### Technical Must-Haves
- **JavaScript & React** - Appears in almost every frontend role
- **Algorithm Design** - Ability to analyze systems and develop solutions
- **Testing Experience** - Understanding of automated testing frameworks
- **Problem-Solving Skills** - Define logic and tackle complex challenges

## How to Stand Out

### Before You Apply
1. **Build a React Project** - Show mastery of their preferred framework
2. **Study Their Case Studies** - Understand the types of clients and challenges
3. **Optimize Your Resume** - Use keywords like "React," "JavaScript," "systems analysis"

### What Impresses Them
- **Full-Stack Portfolio** - Demonstrate end-to-end development skills
- **Business Thinking** - Show you understand how technology drives value
- **Client Focus** - Remember, you're joining a consulting company

## The Reality of Working There

### What Graduates Love
- **Rapid Learning** - Enterprise-level challenges from day one
- **Project Variety** - Different industries and technologies constantly
- **Career Growth** - Clear paths from junior to technical leadership
- **Global Opportunities** - Work across multiple countries

### What to Expect
- **Client-Facing Work** - You'll present solutions and gather requirements
- **Fast-Paced Environment** - Consulting means tight deadlines
- **Continuous Learning** - Technologies evolve quickly

## Application Strategy

### Timeline
- **Q4 2024 - Q1 2025** - Peak recruitment for graduate programs
- **Rolling Admissions** - Strong candidates get quick decisions

### Red Flags to Avoid
- Generic applications without customization
- Focusing only on coding without business context
- Ignoring their client-consulting model
