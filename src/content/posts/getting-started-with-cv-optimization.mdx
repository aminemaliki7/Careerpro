# CV Optimization: Get More Interviews in 2025

Your CV has **6 seconds** to make an impression. Here’s how to get past ATS systems and get noticed by recruiters.  

---

## The Reality Check

- Most CVs are rejected automatically by ATS  
- Many jobs have hundreds of applicants  
- **Keywords are more important than fancy design**  

---

## CV Structure

**Header (Simple Contact Info)**  
- Name  
- Job title  
- Email | Phone | LinkedIn/GitHub  
- Location  

**Professional Summary (2-3 lines)**  
- Job title and experience  
- Key achievements with numbers  
- Target role  

**Skills Section**  
- List exactly what job descriptions ask for  

---

## 3 Critical Rules

1. **Match Keywords**: Include repeated keywords from target job postings  
2. **Use Numbers**: Quantify achievements wherever possible  
3. **Keep Format Simple**: PDF only, standard fonts, bullet points, no tables or graphics  

---

## Quick Tips

**Do This:**  
- File name: FirstName_LastName_CV.pdf  
- Clear section headers: Experience, Education, Skills  
- Include contact info in the body  
- Proofread  

**Avoid This:**  
- Photos, colors, or fancy fonts  
- “References available upon request”  
- Generic objectives  

---

## Test Your CV

- Use tools like Jobscan to check keyword matching  
- Copy your CV to Notepad — if it looks messy, ATS will struggle  

---

## Action Plan

1. Update professional summary with numbers  
2. Add relevant keywords from job descriptions  
3. Test with Jobscan  
4. Apply to a few positions to test results  

---

🚀 **Bottom line:** A simple, clear, and keyword-optimized CV can **dramatically increase your interview chances**.
