## AI Hype: The New Dotcom?

In the 2000s, the internet changed everything.  
But before Amazon and Google took off, there was a **dotcom bubble**: overfunding, overhiring, and sudden collapse.  

Today, **AI feels the same**. Billions are being invested worldwide, but companies act differently when it comes to hiring.  

👉 Instead of **overhiring**, many are **holding back**, hoping AI will replace jobs.  

## The “Reverse Bubble Pop”

Uncle Bob, a well-known figure in software engineering, predicts a **reverse bubble pop**:  

- Instead of layoffs after overhiring, we’ll see a **rush to hire** after underhiring.  
- When businesses realize AI can’t fully replace engineers, they’ll need **human talent** again — and fast.  

## What This Means for Morocco 🇲🇦

Morocco is in a unique position:  

- Thousands of **young engineers** graduate every year from schools like ENSIAS, INPT, MIAGE, FST, and 1337.  
- **Outsourcing and nearshore** companies in Casablanca, Rabat, and Technopark depend on international demand.  
- Right now, many firms are cautious about hiring because of AI expectations.  

But when the correction comes, Morocco could benefit from a **sudden surge in demand** for developers, testers, and data specialists.  

## Advice for Moroccan Developers

- **Stay ready**: keep learning AI, cloud, backend, testing, and DevOps.  
- **Build a portfolio**: side projects, open source, freelancing.  
- **Think global**: Moroccan talent is competitive internationally — remote jobs are growing.  

---

🚀 **Bottom line**: Don’t be discouraged by today’s slow hiring. The “reverse bubble pop” could open massive opportunities for Moroccan developers — just like the post-dotcom era created global tech giants.
