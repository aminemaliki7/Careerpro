## AI Hype: The New Dotcom?

In the early 2000s, the internet seemed to change everything overnight. Investors poured billions into startups, teams expanded rapidly, and the world was convinced that the next big thing was just around the corner. But then reality hit. The **dotcom bubble** burst, leaving many overhired companies struggling and careers disrupted.  

Fast forward to 2025, and the feeling is familiar. **Artificial intelligence is the new internet** in terms of hype. Billions are flowing into AI startups and research, yet when it comes to hiring, many companies are acting with caution.  

Instead of overstaffing, some organizations are hesitating to bring in new talent, assuming AI might do the work of humans. This cautious approach creates a curious paradox: the technology promises growth, but hiring is temporarily frozen.

## The “Reverse Bubble Pop”

Veteran software engineer Uncle Bob calls it a **reverse bubble pop**. Unlike the dotcom era where companies laid off workers after overhiring, the AI era may see companies **scrambling to hire** after initially underestimating the need for human expertise.  

When businesses realize AI cannot fully replace engineers, developers, testers, and data specialists will suddenly become highly sought after. The talent that was once overlooked will become critical for companies aiming to scale responsibly.

## Why Morocco Could Benefit

Morocco sits in a unique position in the global tech ecosystem. Every year, thousands of engineers graduate from institutions such as ENSIAS, INPT, MIAGE, FST, and 1337. Many of these graduates are eager to work on international projects through outsourcing and nearshore companies located in Casablanca, Rabat, and Technopark.  

Currently, hiring is cautious as firms wait to understand AI’s real impact. However, when the market corrects itself, Morocco could experience a surge in demand for skilled professionals. Developers, testers, and data specialists could find opportunities both locally and remotely, with Moroccan talent competing globally.

## How Developers Can Prepare

To take advantage of what might come next, developers should focus on continuous growth. Learning AI tools, cloud technologies, backend frameworks, testing, and DevOps will strengthen their profiles. Building a strong portfolio through side projects, open source contributions, or freelancing can make a candidate more attractive. Thinking globally is essential, as remote work opens doors to international opportunities.

## Final Thoughts

Today’s hiring landscape may feel slow, but patience and preparation could pay off significantly. The **reverse bubble pop** could transform the job market in Morocco, creating new opportunities for those who are ready to embrace them. Just like the post-dotcom era eventually gave rise to global tech giants, Moroccan developers have a chance to ride the next wave of technology-driven growth.
