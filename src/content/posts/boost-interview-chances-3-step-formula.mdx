You send 50 applications. You hear from 2 companies.

Here's the brutal truth: **75% of resumes never reach human eyes.** They're rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before any recruiter sees them.

But smart candidates are getting 5x more interviews using a simple 3-step optimization process.

## The 3-Step Formula for More Interviews

### Step 1: Upload Resume - See What's Broken

Most resumes fail because they're not ATS-compatible. The software can't read fancy formatting or doesn't find the right keywords.

**Quick fixes that work:**
- Use simple formatting (no tables, graphics, or columns)
- Include exact keywords from job descriptions
- Quantify your achievements with numbers
- Save as .docx or .pdf format

**Example transformation:**
- **Before:** "Experienced developer"
- **After:** "React.js developer with 3+ years experience building web applications"

### Step 2: Add Job - Match Requirements Precisely

Generic resumes don't work. Each application needs to mirror the specific job language.

**The smart approach:**
1. Copy the job description
2. Identify repeated keywords and skills
3. Update your resume to include these exact terms
4. Focus on requirements they mention multiple times

### Step 3: View Results - Optimize Based on Data

Most people guess what works. Successful candidates measure and improve.

**Track these metrics:**
- Response rate by resume version
- Interview requests per 10 applications
- Time from application to callback
