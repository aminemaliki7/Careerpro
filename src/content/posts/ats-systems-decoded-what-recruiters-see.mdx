# ATS Systems Decoded: What Happens After You Hit Submit

Ever wonder why you never hear back after applying? Here's exactly what happens to your CV in the 30 seconds after you submit it.

## The Reality: ATS Systems Aren't That Smart

**The Myth:** ATS systems use AI to perfectly understand your qualifications.

**The Truth:** They're glorified databases doing basic keyword matching. Simple to beat once you know how.

## Your CV's 30-Second Journey

1. **File Processing (5 seconds)** - Converts your PDF to plain text
2. **Data Extraction (10 seconds)** - Pulls out contact info, dates, job titles
3. **Keyword Matching (10 seconds)** - Counts matches against job requirements
4. **Scoring (5 seconds)** - Assigns compatibility percentage

**Critical fact:** Only CVs scoring 70%+ reach human recruiters.

## What Recruiters Actually See

Instead of your nicely formatted CV, recruiters see a dashboard like this:

```
CANDIDATE NAME    | SCORE | LOCATION | EXPERIENCE | STATUS
John Smith        | 87%   | SF, CA   | 5 years    | New
Sarah Johnson     | 92%   | Remote   | 3 years    | Reviewed  
Mike Chen         | 43%   | NYC      | 7 years    | Rejected
```

**Key insight:** Recruiters spend 15 seconds scanning this list. Your score determines if they click on your profile.

## The 5 Things That Kill Your ATS Score

### 1. Wrong Format
**Problem:** Contact info in header/footer, fancy formatting
**Fix:** Put everything in the main document body, use simple layout

### 2. Creative Section Names
**Problem:** "Where I Made Impact" instead of "Experience"
**Fix:** Use standard headers: Experience, Skills, Education

### 3. Missing Keywords
**Problem:** Job wants "React" but you wrote "JavaScript framework"
**Fix:** Use exact words from job descriptions

### 4. Bad File Format
**Problem:** .docx files or corrupted PDFs
**Fix:** Save as PDF from Word or Google Docs (desktop version)

### 5. Weird Fonts/Bullets
**Problem:** Custom bullets (★, ►) or decorative fonts
**Fix:** Arial/Calibri fonts, standard bullets (•, -)

## The ATS-Proof CV Template

### Header Format
```
JOHN SMITH
Senior Software Engineer
john.smith@email.com | (555) 123-4567
linkedin.com/in/johnsmith | github.com/johnsmith
San Francisco, CA
```

### Skills Section (Match Job Descriptions Exactly)
```
TECHNICAL SKILLS
Programming Languages: JavaScript, Python, TypeScript
Frontend: React, Vue.js, Angular, HTML5, CSS3
Backend: Node.js, Express, Django
Database: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis
Cloud: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes
```

### Professional Summary Formula
```
[Job Title] with [X years] experience in [Key Technologies].
[One quantified achievement]. Seeking [target role].
```

**Example:**
> "Full-Stack Developer with 5+ years in React and Node.js. Built applications serving 100K+ users with 99% uptime. Seeking Senior Frontend Engineer roles."

## Quick ATS Optimization Checklist

**Before applying:**
- [ ] Use keywords from job description 5-8 times
- [ ] Standard section headers (Experience, Skills, Education)
- [ ] Contact info in document body, not header
- [ ] PDF format, standard fonts
- [ ] File name: FirstName_LastName_CV.pdf
