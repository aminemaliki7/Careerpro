### How to Build a Career That Thrives in the Age of AI

AI is advancing faster than most careers. David Patterson, a pioneer in computer architecture, recently said:

> “The top AI model is already smarter than 85% of humans. By 2026, 99.9%.”

Employers are noticing skill gaps, and employability is dropping in many regions. So, how do you future-proof your career in this AI-driven world?

Here’s a **three-step roadmap** to help you build a sustainable career, previously shared only with our Growth community of 4,500 members.



### Step 1: Focus on the Right Skills

Businesses pay for **solving critical problems**, whether in product development, marketing, finance, or operations. The closer you are to solving high-impact business problems, the more value—and reward—you create.

Patterns emerge:  
- **2015:** E-commerce boomed. Skilled software engineers were scarce, so salaries skyrocketed.  
- **2020:** Creator economy exploded. Distribution skills became highly valuable. Top creators earned millions because they understood attention and reach.  
- **2025:** AI integration is the new gold rush. Companies are desperate to automate processes. AI experts command top salaries.

**Rule of thumb:** **Business demand + Rare skill = Exponential reward.**



### Step 2: Pick the Right Opportunities

Not all opportunities are equal. The real growth comes from **small, fast-moving startups**, not large corporations.

- **Equity matters:** Employee stock options let you share in the company’s upside.  
- **Experience matters:** Even if a startup fails, the skills and exposure you gain are invaluable.

Example:  
- **Kiran:** Moves across large companies, gets 15% raises each year, but limited value creation.  
- **Krishna:** Bets on a small tech startup, gains deep experience, learns distribution and product skills—even if the startup fails.

One year at the right startup can equal 3–4 years of learning at a large company.



### Step 3: Stay the Course

It’s not just about picking the right opportunity—it’s about **staying long enough to compound your experience**.

Ask yourself:  
1. Is the founding team strong, with previous successes or deep expertise?  
2. Does your role impact core business metrics?  
3. Are you learning consistently?

If the answer is yes, stay. Random job-hopping prevents real skill compounding.



### The Takeaway

- Solve **high-value business problems** with rare skills.  
- Bet on **opportunities that maximize learning and wealth creation**.  
- **Commit long-term** to the right bets to compound experience.

Career growth in the AI era isn’t about following a traditional path—it’s about **strategic bets, rare skills, and continuous learning**.

The individuals and companies who embrace this early will gain a massive advantage in productivity, innovation, and career outcomes.
